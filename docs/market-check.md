# Duplicate / market check — 2026-09-18

This is not an empty category. The idea is only interesting if Math That Matters keeps a specific combined wedge.

## Close products

### Zetamac + newer clones
Strong at fast arithmetic, explicit ranges and keyboard flow. Newer clones add per-question timing, slowest-question analysis and operation-level diagnostics.

Examples:
- https://arithmetic.zetamac.com/
- https://github.com/VedanthR5/v2v-Zetamac
- https://aidenseo.com/zetamac/

### Mental Math Pro
Already offers percentage drilling, number-range configuration and auto-advance.
- https://mentalmathpro.com/mental-math-practice/percentages

### Case Math Practice
Very close on useful interview math topics: percentages, fractions, ratios, percentage changes, reverse percentages, estimation and multi-step work.
- https://casemathpractice.com/mental-math

### ProfitReps
The closest product-direction overlap found. It targets numerical fluency for entrepreneurs/operators/finance, adapts to weak skills, includes business interpretation and decision reps, and has a 60-second speed mode. It is advertised as coming soon to the App Store.
- https://www.profitreps.app/

### Tally / Euclid / Math Trainer
Adaptive mental-math products already cover percentages/fractions and speed/accuracy tracking.
- https://mathtrainer.ai/

## Defensible product wedge to test

Do not position this as merely "useful mental math."

Test this combination instead:

1. Zetamac-level explicit parameter control, including independent ranges.
2. Same skill deliberately repeated under multiple contexts: pure math, startup, finance, poker and CS.
3. Transparent problem-level diagnostics: wrong, slow, corrected, skill and response time.
4. Adaptive selection that is visible and overridable rather than a black-box difficulty ladder.
5. User-customizable workspace/page without turning the app into a theme toy.
6. Free, browser-first, keyboard-first and local-first.

## Idea-basket status

**Keep for validation, not unique/uncontested.**

The broad category is validated and crowded. ProfitReps and Case Math Practice mean "applied useful math" by itself is not a sufficient wedge. The strongest experiment is whether advanced configurability plus cross-context transfer and transparent diagnostics creates a workflow existing tools do not serve well.
